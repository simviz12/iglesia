## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a balance between timeless liturgical solemnity and contemporary pastoral warmth. Designed for an active modern parish community, it invites parishioners, seekers, and families into an experience that feels sacred, dignified, and distinctly human.

The aesthetic philosophy draws heavily from **contemporary editorial design** fused with sacred tradition:
- **Quiet Reverence:** Ample atmospheric whitespace reminiscent of high stone naves, natural materials, and quiet cloisters.
- **Architectural Harmony:** Structural balance, strict typographic proportion, and subtle structural hairline borders that evoke stone masonry and gilded bookbinding.
- **Warm Welcome:** Avoid cold, corporate sterility. Textures evoke parchment, unpolished alabaster, warm candle glow, and rich sacramental vestments.
- **Intimacy & Community:** Interactive elements remain understated, tactile, and deliberate, treating announcements, prayer intentions, and sacraments with thoughtful gravity rather than transactional urgency.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in sacred architecture, liturgical vestments, and illuminated manuscripts:

- **Primary (`#821E29` - Cardinal Red):** A profound liturgical crimson, representing sacrifice, profound love, and spiritual commitment. Used for key calls to action, active sacrament states, primary badges, and solemn highlights.
- **Secondary (`#1B2A4A` - Midnight Nave):** A deep contemplative night blue echoing stained glass in twilight and Mary's mantle. Used for primary typography, solemn headers, and evening vigil events.
- **Tertiary (`#C99A45` - Altar Gold):** A restrained, matte antique gold. Used deliberately for delicate hairlines, lit candles/intentions, sacramental badges, and ornamental framing accents. Never reflective or brassy.
- **Neutral Base (`#F8F6F0` - Alabaster & Parchment):** The foundation of the light theme. A soft, warm ivory that prevents eye strain, with layered step-downs (`#F2EFE8` for structural containers, `#E8E4DA` for low-contrast borders, and `#FAF8F4` for elevated surfaces).
- **Ink & Body (`#2B2824` - Lampblack Charcoal):** High-readability softened black for body typography and long-form parish letters.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical intellectual presence with pristine modern legibility:

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in **Playfair Display**. It carries classic transitions, delicate serifs, and high-contrast grace that evokes both historical missals and modern editorial prestige. Used for liturgical seasons, parish mottoes, and solemn headings.
- **Body & Functional UI:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its rounded, open letterforms balance the sharpness of the serif with human hospitality, clarity, and exceptional digital legibility across dense schedules, homilies, and bulletin announcements.
- **Editorial Sub-rules:** Liturgical feast headers and uppercase subtitles should carry open letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) with smaller label sizes, evoking traditional stone inscriptions and Roman capitals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an editorial column architecture rooted in generous margins, breathing room, and deliberate vertical pacing:

- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column dynamic grid on desktop (max width `1280px` centered), collapsing to 6 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Pacing Rhythm:** Content sections are framed with spacious vertical margins (`space-2xl`) to avoid cluttered church bulletin aesthetics. Information is delivered in curated, digestible segments.
- **Dynamic Timetable Grid:** The Mass & Liturgy schedule uses an asymmetrical split layout: sticky contextual navigation (Sunday vs. Weekday vs. Confessions) on one flank, with an open liturgical agenda on the opposite pane.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered natural materials and soft, warm light rather than heavy digital drop shadows:

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary mechanism of depth. Lower surfaces use matte stone/parchment (`#F2EFE8`), elevated cards rest on crisp alabaster (`#FFFFFF` or `#FAF8F4`), creating tactile contrast.
- **Candlelit Ambient Shadows:** When floating elements appear (e.g., active sacrament modals, dropdown menus), they utilize a warm, diffused shadow tinted with amber undertones: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(43, 40, 36, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(201, 154, 69, 0.06)`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** All cards and dividers feature ultra-fine 1px borders in soft gold or stone dust (`#E5DFD3` or `rgba(201, 154, 69, 0.25)`), offering architectural structure reminiscent of bound parchment volumes.

## Shapes

The shape system employs disciplined, soft architectural contours:

- **Soft Geometry (`roundedness: 1`):** Buttons, cards, and input fields use modest `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii. This preserves the dignified, timeless structure of books and sanctuary architectural lines.
- **Exceptions for Sacred Focus:** Circular avatars and prayer vigil badge indicators use `pill` or full `50%` circles to evoke halos, medals, and communion hosts.
- **Framing Details:** Selected feature cards incorporate subtle inset hairline borders (2px inside the outer container) to mimic traditional liturgical certificates and prayer cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Cardinal Solen):** Deep `#821E29` fill with ivory `#F8F6F0` text. Soft 4px radius. Hover transition deepens tone to `#68161F` with a soft 1px gold ring outline.
- **Secondary (Midnight Contemplative):** `#1B2A4A` fill with white text for official administrative or parish registration actions.
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Transparent background with a 1px border in Altar Gold (`#C99A45`) and Cardinal or Midnight text. Hover introduces a warm ivory fill (`#FAF5EC`).

### Cards & Liturgical Timetable Modules
- **Sacramental & Announcement Cards:** Solid ivory/alabaster base, framed by a soft `1px` border of `rgba(201, 154, 69, 0.25)`. Includes an optional top decorative accent line in Cardinal Red or Altar Gold.
- **Mass Schedule Cards:** Arranged horizontally or in timeline stacks. Highlighting the upcoming service with a soft gold border and an illuminated dot indicator. Includes clear distinction for language, rite, and celebrant.

### Prayer & Community Modules ("Llamadas a la Oración")
- **Intention & Intercession Blocks:** Elegant card with italicized serif script quotation, subtle warm background (`#F5EFE6`), and interactive "Unirse en Oración" (Join in Prayer) counter with candle glow animation.

### Donation & Tithing Blocks ("Ofrenda & Caritas")
- **Frequency & Amount Selectors:** Compact pill buttons with warm muted stone backgrounds when idle; active amounts turn into solid Cardinal Red with ivory typography. Clear explanatory text regarding parish community initiatives (charity, temple maintenance, pastoral ministry).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat off-white surface, `1px` neutral border (`#D8D2C5`). Focus state transitions smoothly to an Altar Gold hairline (`#C99A45`) without garish neon glows, accompanied by clean label typography in Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Chips & Liturgical Tags
- Categorical chips for Liturgical Seasons (Advent, Lent, Ordinary Time, Easter) using delicate tinted backgrounds with coordinating text and small 6px gold/ruby status pips.
